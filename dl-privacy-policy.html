Privacy Policy

App: DL Status Nepal

Effective date: 26 April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DL Status Nepal ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the DL Status Nepal mobile application (the "App").

DL Status Nepal is an unofficial app. Data shown in the app is fetched from government-related data sources through third-party API infrastructure.

1. Information We Collect

We may process the following information:

  • License number you enter in the app to check status.
  • Recent search history stored locally on your device (up to 10 recent entries).
  • Basic diagnostic and technical information generated by your device and third-party services (such as ad services).

2. How We Use Information

  • To fetch and display driving license print status results.
  • To improve app stability, reliability, and user experience.
  • To show advertisements in supported versions of the app.

3. Local Storage

The app stores recent search items on your device using local storage. This data is not automatically uploaded by the app to our own servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (for ads)
  • Google Play Services
  • API endpoint service used to fetch license status data

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with third-party service providers only as needed to operate core app features (for example, status lookup and ad delivery).

6. Data Retention

  • Recent searches remain on your device until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or remove history from within the app (if available).
  • Third-party services may retain technical logs based on their own policies.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data in transit and at rest where applicable. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Choices

  • You may stop using the app at any time.
  • You can clear local app data from your device settings.
  • You can control certain ad personalization settings through your Google account and device settings.
