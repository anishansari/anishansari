How SCADA Works

StepWorking Mechanism
1. Data CollectionSensors measure level, pressure, temperature, flow, voltage, and equipment state.
2. PLC ProcessingPLC receives sensor signals and executes ladder logic/control algorithms.
3. CommunicationData travels over industrial networks using Modbus TCP, OPC UA, Ethernet/IP, DNP3, or IEC-104.
4. SCADA SupervisionSCADA server monitors the entire plant in real time and raises alarms.
5. Historian LoggingAll telemetry and alarms are stored for trend analysis and reporting.
6. Operator ActionOperators use HMI screens to acknowledge alarms and control equipment remotely.
7. Automated ResponsePLC can automatically stop pumps, close valves, or trigger shutdowns during faults.
